0 · The Purpose of This Guide

This document is the Architect’s own record of how the Room was built and why each piece exists. It preserves the logic and the process — not merely the result — so the room can be maintained, extended, or rebuilt from first principles by anyone the Architect trusts. It reveals the architecture of the room fully. What it does not touch is the family’s sealed core, which this guide neither needs nor exposes.

1 · The Concept — One Sentence, One Law

The room began as a single sentence: any honest calculation becomes a structure. Everything in the Calculator serves that sentence. A student types mathematics on a keypad; if the calculation is honest, the room gives it a body, a family, a mass, a place on the floor, a shadow answer, a reflection answer, and — when commanded — motion that obeys law. If the calculation is not honest, the room refuses it, and says why in teaching language. The room never silently fixes, never guesses, never decorates. It admits, or it teaches.

2 · The Chamber — the Fixed Stage

The room is one fixed chamber, never resized, never re-lit by accident. Its coordinates are the stage on which every figure performs:

ElementSetting
Half-widthW = 13.5 — the walls stand at ±13.5 on their axes
Floorthe plane y = 0; zero belongs to it alone
Floor skintranslucent cerulean laced with gold
Wallslapis, veined with gold
North walla mirror, corner to corner, holding the whole room flipped
GravityG = 13 — real in the room, scaled for the eye

Fixing the stage was a deliberate architectural decision: a lawful world needs constant addresses. Every shadow, reflection, bounce, and roll is computable precisely because the stage never moves.

3 · The Keypad Grammar — Honest Calculations Only

Entry is by bare numbers and the variables x, y, z — a ruling the Architect made deliberately, so that students practice building shapes rather than invoking them. A single parser, parseEq, decides what a string is allowed to mean. The grammar is closed and has never been altered since it was sealed:

The student writesThe room answers
x² + y² + z² = kthe sphere, closed
x² + y² = kthe cylinder
x²/a + y²/b + z²/c = kthe ellipsoid — all three terms must be fractions
z = a(x² + y²)the paraboloid
the torus grammarthe ring, with its hole handed back to the floor
a complex entrythe twofold — two manifolds, one seam
plain arithmetica number, honored as itself
anything elsethe wonder — the refusal that teaches
THE WONDER LAW. An entry the grammar cannot honor does not error. The room answers with the wonder — a structured refusal that names what was wrong and what would make it right. A wrong answer is returned to the law, never punished.

4 · The Closure Law — Every Figure a Closed Body

The deepest structural law of the room. A figure enters at the room’s center of attention; before it is admitted, the closure pass measures its extents along its own axes. Any axis thinner than 42% of the greatest extent is grown until it reaches 42% — never leaving the figure flat, never letting a degenerate body exist in the world. After the repair, every extent is capped at 0.6 of the maximum so the room holds its scale.

closure pass  —  minExtent / maxExtent  >=  0.42   (the floor of body)
                —  uniform cap at 0.6          (the room keeps its scale)
                —  repaired == true            (the repair is spoken, never silent)
THE SPOKEN REPAIR. When the closure pass acts, the room says so, in teaching language: the shape arrived flat, and the room closed it lawfully. A silent repair would teach that flat is acceptable. The spoken repair teaches the opposite. This distinction is not polish — it is pedagogy enforced by architecture.

5 · The Families — Read From the Geometry

Nothing in the room is labeled by hand. Every admitted figure is classified by reading its own geometry, and the family decides how it lives — how it bounces, rolls, floats, or crawls.

FamilyThe read that establishes itHow it lives
planarthin in one axis with a long middle run, or extremely thin overallfloats at a set height, bobs gently, travels on spin
peaka lonely apex cluster above a base — a pyramid, a tetrahedrongrounded, crawls, bounces highest of all
rounda dense mesh — the ball, the spheregrounded, rolls, bounces lively
blockeverything else with bodygrounded, tumbles, rolls poorly

Two computed properties govern the round family’s life: q, the aspect ratio of the figure’s greatest to least extent, and peakK, the height of a peak over its own base. The closer q sits to 1, the truer the ball — and the faster it rolls. A squashed sphere is a slow sphere; the room knows this from the geometry, not from a table.

6 · The Physics — Gravity, Friction, Rest

Motion is integrated on a clamped timestep and obeys a small set of lawful constants:

gravity     G = 13, always downward
friction    grounded: strong   floating: gentle
bounce      round, fast-falling:  lively  (−0.62)
            everything else:      subdued (−0.34)
walls       limit by radius; ricochet × −0.72; upward pop if fast
rest        when the fall is small and the drift is less — the body sleeps

Planar figures are the exception the law accounts for: they do not fall. They ride a float height set from their radius, bob on a slow sine, drift with strong damping, and are clamped inside the walls with a soft ricochet and a spin kick. Nothing escapes the chamber; nothing falls through the floor.

7 · The Energy-Transfer Law — the Ball Gives Its Motion

The Architect’s own physics, stated in one sentence and carried out exactly: the ball bounces off any object; if the object is lighter or smaller, the ball propels it — transfers the energy — stops, and the object continues; then the ball rolls just a little.

ball law  —  if the ball is moving and the target is lighter (mass < 95% of the ball)
                or smaller (radius < 95% of the ball):
                the target receives the motion: velocity += heading × speed × 1.02
                the ball keeps 7% and stops. Then it rolls a little.

Measured in the verification battery after a dash-phase collision: the struck cube carried 1.71 of velocity; the ball kept 0.12 and stopped. The law is not flavor text — it is a number the harness checks every run.

8 · The Three Functions — BOUNCE · ROLL · DASH

Three buttons give the student command over the figures, and each family answers differently — the same command, answered according to what the thing is:

BOUNCEtravels on its spin−(6.5 + 2.4·peakK), capped at 10 — the harder the peak, the higher−(6.5 + 1.2/q)a toss and a tumble
ROLLspins in placecrawls at 1.6min(8, 4.6·(1.5/q)) with true rolling rotationtumbles at 2.2

The bounce ordering is itself a law: a peak must out-bounce a ball, because the harder the peak the higher it rises — verified, 8.0 against 7.7. Dash power scales with proximity to a surface and is capped at 18, so even the great plate cannot become a projectile of unlimited force.

9 · The Lights — Three Stations, Nine Coordinates

The room’s light is not ambient; it is placed. Three stations, each fully addressed by its three coordinates, chosen by the student and spoken by the narrator when changed:

StationCoordinatesCharacter
L₁(−4.2, 4.6, 2.6)high, left-rear — the long shadow, thrown right-forward
L₂(4.2, 4.6, 2.6)high, right-rear — the mirrored long shadow, thrown left-forward
L₃(0, 3.4, 4.4)low, frontal — the shadow thrown straight behind, stretched long

Shadows are cast by true rays from the light through every vertex of every figure — the footprint is the figure’s own silhouette, answered on the floor. The Architect ruled that the light system stands as designed: each lesson keeps its focus, and a fuller illumination-trajectory law waits for its own chapter.

10 · The Golden Frame — the Silhouette, Ennobled

Every figure in the room wears a thin gold frame on its silhouette. Architecturally, the frame is computed by building a face-incidence map keyed on quantized vertex positions: an edge used by exactly one face, or whose two faces straddle the view direction, is a silhouette edge and is stroked gold. Quantization matters — mesh seams positionally coincident must not read as boundaries, or a sphere would wear a gold lattice instead of a gold outline. Drawn after the solid body, at full strength in the room and softened in the mirror, the frame marks each figure as admitted, closed, and the room’s own.

11 · The Mirror Wall — the Room, Answered

The entire north wall is a mirror, corner to corner, holding the whole room flipped. Every figure, every shadow answer, every gold frame is answered across the plane — the same objects, coordinate by coordinate, never imitations. The mirror is the room’s permanent demonstration of the family’s fourth law: a whole object registers itself on every plane it meets — across it, and on it.

13 · The Verification Process — How Every Law Is Tested

The room ships with a harness — an external battery of scripted scenarios driven against a copy of the app, each asserting the laws numerically and reporting a single verdict: CLEAN or the exact failure. The battery checks, every run:

closure    —  closed == true, min depth == 0.42        (the floor of body)
families   —  round, block, peak, planar all established
bounce     —  peak 8.0  ·  ball 7.7  ·  block 6.5          (the ordering holds)
roll       —  ball 6.8  ·  cube 2.2                       (the ball rolls fastest)
float      —  the plate rides at 1.72, inside the walls
transfer   —  cube 1.71  ·  ball 0.12                      (the energy law, measured)
rest       —  every body settles and sleeps
wonder     —  the refusal teaches, and is never silent
repair     —  the flat arrival is closed AND spoken
exports    —  all three carry out with correct names

No change ships while any scenario reports otherwise. This process — not optimism — is why the room’s claims and its behavior are the same object.
